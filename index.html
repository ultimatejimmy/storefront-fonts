Licensing, Open Access & Legal

100% open-source, non-commercial community initiative for e-reader typography.

Open Font Licensing

All fonts in the Storefront catalog are distributed strictly in compliance with their respective open licenses—principally the SIL Open Font License (OFL 1.1), Apache License 2.0, and MIT License. Every font package archive distributed by this catalog includes its original unmodified license file and copyright notice.

No File Submissions Policy

To eliminate licensing ambiguity and prevent copyright infringement, community members suggest fonts by submitting the upstream public repository and license metadata. Maintainers inspect upstream repositories, verify the license headers, and build the packages and subsetted previews directly from official sources.

DMCA & Takedown Notices

If you believe a font has been included in error or without appropriate open-license attribution, please open an issue with the dmca-takedown label or email the project maintainer. Verified requests are processed within 24–48 hours.
